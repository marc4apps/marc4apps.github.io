Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android apps published by MARC APPS ("we", "us", "our"). Please read it carefully. By installing or using our apps, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the apps.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data. We do not run our own servers for your data, we do not require you to create an account, and the content you work with in our apps (such as photos, videos, or settings) stays on your device.

Advertising and third-party services

Our apps are free and show ads through third-party advertising networks. These networks are independent from us and may collect and use data from your device in order to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. The networks we use are:

  • Google AdMob – how AdMob uses data and Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Audience Network (Facebook) – Meta Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads – Unity Privacy Policy

The data these services may collect includes, for example, your advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, screen size, app usage and ad interaction information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. We do not receive this data and have no control over how these companies use it. We encourage you to read their privacy policies linked above.

Your choices about ads

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (usually under Settings → Google → Ads, or the equivalent on your device).
  • Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area or the UK, an app may ask for your consent before ads are personalized, and you may be able to change that choice.
  • You can also manage ad preferences directly with each ad network using the links above.

Log data and cookies

Our apps do not use cookies directly. The third-party advertising services above may use cookies, identifiers, or similar technologies, and may collect technical information (such as IP address, device information, and time of use) when ads are loaded or when an error occurs in their code.

Permissions

Some apps ask for Android permissions (for example camera, microphone, storage, or display over other apps) only so that their features can work. Data accessed through these permissions is processed on your device and is not sent to us.

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided personal information to an advertising service through one of our apps, please contact us and we will help as best we can.

Data deletion

Because we do not collect or store your personal data, there is nothing for us to delete. Data held by the advertising networks can be managed or deleted through their own privacy tools, linked above. Data stored inside an app on your device can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Security

Since your data stays on your device, its security depends mainly on your device's own protections. We cannot guarantee the security of data handled by third-party services.
